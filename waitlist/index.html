Why I'm building this

I've watched 15,000 students stand 200 metres apart doing the exact same boring evening, every day, because nobody had a way to shout "I'm going right now — who else?"

Showup is that shout. We're launching with one campus and making sure the first people in never open an empty app. If you sign up early, you're not a user — you're setting the culture.
